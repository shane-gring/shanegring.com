## A website describes. An operating site runs.

The standard agency website is a brochure: written once, aged
immediately, disconnected from how the business works. It
answers the questions your business got asked three years ago.

An operating site is built from your Operating Memo — from how the business actually runs. Every claim is true and structured so machines can find, understand, and quote it. Every page is a component you can reuse, so the site grows with the business instead of drifting from it. And the whole thing is documented and handed over: yours to run, not ours to hold hostage.

**This is the difference between a site you have and a
surface you operate.**

## What every build includes

### Extraction to launch

We start from your Operating Memo, not a blank Figma file.
Structure, copy, and build all derive from the same source:
how your business works.

### Machine readability throughout

Structured data on every page, citable claims, no facts
trapped in images. When someone asks an AI about your
business, it quotes you instead of guessing.

### A componentized system, not pages

New offer? New page in hours, not a new project. The site
is a kit that matches your business's parts.

### A content system you can run

Publishing, updating, and extending the site is documented
for your team. No retainer required to change a headline.

### The handoff session

90 minutes at launch: how everything works, what needs
tending and how often, and what you can safely ignore. You
leave able to run it.
