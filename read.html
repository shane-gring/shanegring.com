Not what you meant it to say. What it says. A recorded walkthrough of your site through two lenses: how well it carries the way your business actually operates, and what AI engines can read, quote, and get right about you when someone asks.

Your website describes a company that no longer exists.

You've evolved. Your offers sharpened, your process matured, your best clients changed. Your website is still telling the 2022 version of the story.

That used to cost you slowly. Now it costs you twice. Prospects land on the site and bounce off stale language — or they never land at all. They ask an AI, and it answers from your outdated pages, or guesses.

Right now, machines are answering questions about your business. The Read shows you exactly what they're working with.

What you get

A 30 to 40 minute recorded walkthrough

Me, on video, going through your site page by page. Not a template, not a software score. I look at your business the way an operator does, your site the way a machine does, and show you where the two don't match.

A findings memo you can act on the same day

Every issue ranked by effort against impact, three fixes you can make this week without me, and a conservative estimate of what the gaps cost you. Written to work whether or not we ever talk again.

Your AI visibility, on screen

I ask the major AI engines the questions your prospects ask. You watch what comes back. It's the part your partners will want to see.

Every issue ranked by effort against impact, so the argument about what to do first is already settled when you open it.

What I'm looking for

This is not an SEO audit and it is not a tool. Search for “website audit” and you get software: a crawler returns a score, a list of warnings, and no opinion. Those are useful and they are free, and if that is what you want you should use one. Most of them check conversion mechanics too — button placement, headline formulas, page speed. Useful, narrow, and not what this is.

The Read is a person watching your site the way a buyer does and the way a machine does, at the same time, and saying which of the two your business is losing to.

The Read looks at three things:

  • Does the site carry your operating logic? How you actually work, who you actually serve, what actually makes you different. Most sites carry a summary written years ago by someone who no longer works there.
  • Can machines read it? Structure, not just content. Whether your pages let search and AI engines extract, understand, and quote the real story. Most don't, and it's invisible until you look.
  • Does the story hold together? One business, one message, across every page. Or five eras of the company layered on top of each other.

How it works

Step 1

Order and answer five questions

Your URL, who you serve, what you sell, what's changed recently, what prompted this. Ten minutes — the only time I'll ask for.

Step 2

I do the work

Your site, your structured data, your AI and search footprint, your closest competitors' footprint for contrast.

Step 3

You get the Read

Video and memo in your inbox within 2 business days. Watch on your own time; share with whoever needs it.

Optional: a 20-minute debrief. A booking link comes in the delivery email, good for two weeks, included in the price. Most people won't need it.

Who this is for

Founder-led businesses doing roughly $1M to $10M, where the real story of the company lives in the founder's head and the website hasn't caught up. Services firms, consultancies, credentialing and standards organizations, specialized B2B.

Who it's not for: pre-revenue startups, sites still under construction, and businesses where the website genuinely doesn't matter to how you win work. If I look at your situation and conclude the Read won't be worth $450 to you, I'll refund you and tell you why.

The guarantee

If the Read doesn't show you something material about your business that you didn't already know, I'll refund the full $450. No form, no friction. Reply to the delivery email and say so.
