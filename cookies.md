# The cookies this site sets.

Which cookies shanegring.com sets, why, and how to turn them off. The site uses analytics cookies only, and no advertising cookies.

Short version: shanegring.com uses analytics cookies to count visits and identification cookies to
recognise business visitors, and no advertising cookies at all. Nothing here shows you ads or follows you
around the web. This page explains what is set and how to switch it off. Last updated 7 October 2026.

## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser so it can recognise a return visit or measure how
the site is used. Some are set by the site you are on; others are set by third-party services the site
relies on. Similar technologies, like local storage, do the same job and are covered here too.

## What this site uses

On the live site (shanegring.com), Google Analytics 4 runs through Google Tag Manager and sets analytics
cookies, typically named `_ga` and `_ga_<id>`. They hold a random identifier and
timestamps so visits can be counted and returning visitors told apart from new ones. They last up to about
two years unless you clear them.

The live site also runs RB2B, a visitor-identification service, through the same tag manager. RB2B sets
its own cookies and local storage holding a random identifier, and combines them with your IP address to
try to match a visit to a public business profile — see the [Privacy](https://shanegring.com/privacy) page for how
that match is used and how to opt out. These are identification cookies, not advertising ones: nothing on
this site shows you ads. RB2B does not load for browsers that send a
Global Privacy Control signal, and preview and local versions of the site set no cookies at all.

The live site also runs the LinkedIn Insight Tag, which sets LinkedIn cookies holding a random
identifier so LinkedIn can report how many people arrived from a post or an ad and which pages they
read. Unlike the two above, this one does feed advertising: LinkedIn can use it to build an audience
of past visitors and show them ads on LinkedIn itself. It does not show ads on this site, and the
reporting Shane sees is counts, never a list of names. Like the rest, it loads on shanegring.com only.

## Third parties

Google provides the analytics and tag services, RB2B provides visitor identification, and LinkedIn
provides the Insight Tag; each processes that data under its own terms. Some pages embed
or link to other services — beehiiv for the newsletter, Cal.com for booking, and the "Add to Preferred
Sources" button that opens Google — and those set their own cookies only once you interact with them on
their own domains. See the [Privacy](https://shanegring.com/privacy) page for what each service handles.

## Turning cookies off

You are in control. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, and most let you refuse
third-party cookies by default. A privacy extension or ad blocker will stop Google Analytics, RB2B, and
the LinkedIn Insight Tag from loading, and the site honours a Global Privacy Control signal where your browser sends one. Blocking these
cookies does not break the site — every page still works the same.
