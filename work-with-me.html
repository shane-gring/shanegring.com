Work with me

Nobody owns the digital side of your business.

Not marketing, not IT, and not the agency that built the site three years ago. It falls to you, on top of everything else, until it stops getting done altogether. Here is what to do about it, depending on how much of it you want to keep holding.

Working together

What do you need someone for?

Four sizes. The question is how much of this you want off your own plate, and for how long.

getting started The Session You have been circling the same decision for weeks. Bring it, and leave knowing what to do about it. building your system The Install You stop waiting on someone else to change anything. By the end of one day you can ship it yourself. maintaining your site The Autopilot The site stops going stale. Someone tends it every month whether or not it is on your mind. enhancing your operations The Seat The way the business runs gets better every month, instead of just staying upright.
Programs

What do you want to walk away with?

Each one leaves you with something you keep, and each credits in full toward the next.

a score The Scan Find out whether there is a problem worth paying anyone to fix. an opinion The Read Stop guessing at what is wrong. You get it named, ranked, and in an order you can act on. a plan The Map Stop rebuilding on hunches. You know what to build, and what it costs, before anyone starts. the thing built The Site The site stops describing the business and starts running it.

The order is the point. A score tells you whether to look, an opinion tells you what is wrong, a plan tells you what to build, and the build is the last step rather than the first.

When none of it is the answer

Four situations where I would tell you to keep your money. They come up often enough to be worth putting on the page.

  • What you actually want is a visual refresh of the site you have
  • The real problem is demand, not operations
  • Nobody inside the business thinks anything needs to change
  • You want the diagnosis, but you do not intend to act on it
